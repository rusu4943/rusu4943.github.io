About me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, advised by Dr. Jin Sun. My research focuses on agentic AI, multimodal reasoning, and generative models, with particular interests in spatial reasoning, LLM/VLM post-training, and efficient diffusion models. I aim to develop AI systems that can reason more effectively, improve through feedback, and operate efficiently.

Prior to my Ph.D., I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, where I was advised by Dr. Mohammad Rostami. My earlier research focused on transfer learning, domain adaptation, and explainable AI, particularly for medical image analysis.
